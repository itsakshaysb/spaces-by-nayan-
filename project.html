A Mumbai Apartment

Apartment · Mumbai

An apartment in Mumbai, reworked around light and a warm, natural material palette.

The brief was calm — a home that feels unhurried, where the materials do the talking and nothing competes for attention. Walnut, book-matched marble, and fluted glass carry the spaces; hand-painted murals give each bedroom its own quiet character.
